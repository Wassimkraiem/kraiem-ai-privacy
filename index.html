kraiem.ai Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

kraiem.ai is used to manage connected Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts.

Data we access

Account and Page details, posts, comments, messages, sender names and IDs, statistics, and the access tokens needed to connect.

How we use it

To publish content, answer comments and messages, and view statistics. We do not sell this data. It may be processed by Meta and by providers that host the app.

Storage

We keep data only as long as needed for these purposes, or until deletion is requested, unless the law requires longer.

Delete your data

Email wassim.kraiem.ess@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your Facebook or Instagram username. We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also remove kraiem.ai in your Facebook settings under Apps and websites.
